AN OUT ORGANIZATION CONTINUOUS INITIATIVE

Uniform Cycle

Give What You’ve Outgrown. Get What You Need. Keep the Cycle Moving.

Uniform Cycle is a continuous school-uniform reuse, exchange, restoration, and redistribution initiative by OUT Organization, designed to ensure that usable school uniforms continue serving students rather than becoming unused or going to waste.

THE IDEA

A uniform can have more than one journey.

Children grow. Families change. Students transfer schools. Uniform requirements change.

As a result, perfectly usable uniforms are often left behind in homes, schools, cupboards, and communities.

Uniform Cycle gives those uniforms another purpose.

A uniform that no longer fits one student may fit another. A uniform from a student transferring to another school may help a new student. A worn uniform may be restored and returned to use.

Rather than treating uniforms as disposable items, Uniform Cycle treats them as resources that can continue circulating within communities.

OUR VISION

A continuous and inclusive global movement.

To build a continuous and inclusive global movement where school uniforms are kept in use, resources are shared responsibly, and students can access the uniforms they need with dignity.

WHY UNIFORM CYCLE?

What one student has outgrown may be exactly what another student needs.

The cycle creates an opportunity for usable resources to continue serving students instead of being left unused.

Uniform Cycle is designed around a simple but powerful principle: resources can continue creating value when communities find practical ways to share them.

Students grow. Families change. School requirements change. Transfers happen. These changes can leave perfectly usable uniforms behind.

Uniform Cycle creates a structured pathway through which suitable uniforms can be contributed, collected, assessed, restored, matched and made available for reuse.

The initiative brings together students, families, schools, organizations, businesses and individuals to help keep uniforms moving through communities.

HOW THE CYCLE WORKS

Keeping the cycle moving.

Every contribution is assessed according to its condition and potential for reuse. Suitable uniforms can then move through the cycle toward another student or family.

01

Give

Students, parents, guardians, schools, organizations, businesses and individuals can donate school uniforms they no longer need.

  • Good and ready-to-use
  • Gently worn
  • In need of minor repairs
  • Potentially repairable
02

Collect

OUT Organization works to collect uniforms and connect donated items with students and families who may need them.

03

Restore

Where practical, uniforms that need attention can be cleaned, repaired, restored or prepared for reuse.

04

Match

Available uniforms are sorted and matched according to practical factors such as school, requirements, size, gender where relevant, condition and availability.

05

Exchange or Request

A student or family may contribute a uniform they no longer need and, where a suitable uniform is available, request or exchange it for one that meets their current needs.

SUPPORTING STUDENTS THROUGH CHANGE

Different circumstances. One continuous cycle.

Uniform Cycle recognizes that students and families experience different changes throughout their educational journey.

Students Who Have Outgrown Their Uniforms

When a child outgrows a uniform, the family can contribute it to Uniform Cycle rather than allowing it to remain unused. If a suitable larger uniform is available, the family may request one.

Students Transferring Schools

A student moving to another school can contribute uniforms from their previous school to the cycle. Where possible, they can then request the appropriate uniform required by their new school.

Families Seeking Uniform Support

Families who need assistance accessing school uniforms may participate by requesting available uniforms through the Uniform Cycle process. Availability will depend on donations and current stock.

A GLOBAL OPPORTUNITY TO MAKE A DIFFERENCE

You do not have to live in Kenya to contribute.

Uniform Cycle is open to support from people and organizations around the world. People everywhere can support the mission in ways that are practical and meaningful to them.

Donating Uniforms

Contribute new, gently used, worn or repairable school uniforms that can potentially be returned to use.

Supporting Restoration

Help provide resources for cleaning, repairing, tailoring and restoring uniforms.

Providing Financial Support

Contribute toward collection, transportation, storage, cleaning, repairs, administration and redistribution.

Supporting Logistics

Help with transportation, collection points, storage, shipping or other practical logistics where appropriate.

Partnering With OUT Organization

Schools, businesses, charities, community organizations, manufacturers, retailers and other institutions can collaborate with Uniform Cycle.

Spreading the Message

Share the initiative with families, schools, communities, organizations and networks that may benefit from or contribute to the cycle.

Creating Local Collection Points

Supporters in different locations may help establish appropriate collection networks that connect uniforms with the wider initiative.

Offering Skills or Services

Tailors, cleaners, transport providers, designers, schools, technology professionals, photographers, communicators and other professionals can contribute their skills and services.

Connecting Resources to Need

Anyone with access to uniforms, resources, partnerships, facilities or opportunities that could strengthen the initiative can help connect those resources to the cause.

Responsible reuse

Every contribution is assessed according to its condition and potential for reuse. Not every donated uniform will necessarily be suitable for reuse. Where practical, suitable items may be cleaned, repaired, restored or prepared for another student. Items that cannot reasonably enter the uniform cycle should be handled responsibly through appropriate alternatives.

OUR GOAL

A continuous uniform-sharing system.

To establish a continuous, sustainable, and accessible uniform-sharing system that connects available school uniforms with students who need them while encouraging communities around the world to participate in the cycle.
