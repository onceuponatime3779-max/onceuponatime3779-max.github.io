OUT Organization Initiative

UNIFORM CYCLE

Give What You’ve Outgrown. Get What You Need. Keep the Cycle Moving.

Uniform Cycle is a continuous school-uniform reuse, exchange, restoration, and redistribution initiative by OUT Organization, designed to ensure that usable school uniforms continue serving students rather than becoming unused or going to waste.

See How It Works Join the Uniform Cycle
About the Initiative

Keeping Uniforms in Use

The initiative creates a sustainable cycle in which students, families, schools, organizations, businesses, and individuals can contribute uniforms they no longer need and help connect them with students who need them.

Instead of allowing usable school uniforms to remain unused, Uniform Cycle creates opportunities for those uniforms to continue serving students and communities.

The Cycle Continues

A uniform that no longer fits one student may fit another. A uniform from a student transferring to another school may help a new student. A worn uniform may be restored and returned to use.

Our Vision

A Continuous Movement

“To build a continuous and inclusive global movement where school uniforms are kept in use, resources are shared responsibly, and students can access the uniforms they need with dignity.”
The Idea

Why Uniform Cycle?

Children grow. Families change. Students transfer schools. Uniform requirements change. As a result, perfectly usable uniforms are often left behind in homes, schools, cupboards, and communities.

Children Grow

A uniform that becomes too small for one student can still be useful to another student.

Students Transfer

Students moving to another school may have uniforms from their previous school that can continue serving others.

Resources Continue

Uniform Cycle treats school uniforms as resources that can continue circulating within communities rather than disposable items.

How It Works

The Uniform Cycle

Every contribution moves through a process designed to keep usable uniforms circulating and connect them with students who may need them.

1

GIVE

Students, parents, guardians, schools, organizations, businesses, and individuals can donate school uniforms they no longer need.

  • Good and ready-to-use
  • Gently worn
  • In need of minor repairs
  • Poor condition but potentially repairable
  • Outgrown uniforms
  • Uniforms from transferred students
2

COLLECT

OUT Organization works to collect uniforms and connect donated items with students and families who may need them.

Supporters may contribute uniforms locally or help facilitate their collection and delivery through appropriate community channels.

3

RESTORE

Where practical, uniforms that need attention can be cleaned, repaired, restored, or prepared for reuse.

This helps extend the useful life of uniforms and allows more students to benefit from every contribution.

4

MATCH

Available uniforms are sorted and matched according to relevant needs and requirements.

  • School
  • Uniform requirements
  • Size
  • Gender where relevant to the uniform design
  • Condition
  • Availability
5

EXCHANGE OR REQUEST

A student or family may contribute a uniform they no longer need and, where a suitable uniform is available, request or exchange it for one that meets their current needs.

6

REUSE

The uniform returns to service and continues its journey from one student, family, or school community to another.

The cycle can begin again when that uniform is no longer needed by its next owner.

Please note: Uniform Cycle does not guarantee that a replacement will always be available. The system depends on what has been donated and what is currently in circulation.
Supporting Students

Supporting Students Through Change

🎒

Students Who Have Outgrown Their Uniforms

When a child outgrows a uniform, the family can contribute it to Uniform Cycle rather than allowing it to remain unused.

If a suitable larger uniform is available, the family may request one.

🏫

Students Transferring Schools

A student moving to another school can contribute uniforms from their previous school to the cycle.

Where possible, they can then request the appropriate uniform required by their new school.

🤝

Families Seeking Uniform Support

Families who need assistance accessing school uniforms may participate by requesting available uniforms through the Uniform Cycle process.

Availability will depend on donations and current stock.

Global Opportunity

A Global Opportunity to Make a Difference

Uniform Cycle is open to support from people and organizations around the world. You do not have to live in Kenya to contribute to this initiative.

Partnering With OUT Organization

Creating Local Collection Points
