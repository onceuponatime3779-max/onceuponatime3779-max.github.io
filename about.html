About OUT Organization | Youth-Led Community Organization in Kenya

From Where We Came, We Rise.

A youth-led organization committed to serving, empowering, and creating opportunities for children, youth, families, and communities in Kenya.

Who We Are

Remembering where we came from and choosing to make a difference.

Once Upon a Time, We Were There Organization , also known as OUT Organization, is a youth-led community organization in Kenya bringing together passionate teenagers and young adults from different higher learning institutions and communities to create positive social impact.

We are driven by compassion, community service, and a desire to uplift vulnerable children, youth, families, and communities.

Our work focuses on creating meaningful opportunities and providing practical support through mentorship, education, humanitarian assistance, talent development, empowerment, environmental conservation, and community outreach.

Our name reflects our connection to the communities we serve. Many of us have seen, experienced, or grown up around challenges affecting children, young people, and families.

We remember where we came from, and that memory inspires us to return, serve, and create opportunities for others.

We were there, and now, we are here to make a difference.

Our Mission

To promote inclusive community development by providing mentorship, educational support, dignity-centered sanitary care, protection from sexual violence, and empowerment programs for children, youth, and families in Kenya, with a commitment to underserved communities.

We engage diverse community members through active participation, volunteerism, and partnerships to create safe, healthy, and hopeful living environments.

Our Vision

A diverse, thriving, and empowered community where all children, youth, and families have equal opportunities, dignity, and support to achieve their full potential.
