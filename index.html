About OUT Organization

From Where We Came, We Rise.

Who We Are

OUT Organization is a community-driven organization committed to creating safe, healthy and hopeful environments for children, youth and families in Mathare.

We engage diverse community members through mentorship, education, community outreach, protection, dignity-centered support and empowerment programs.

Through active participation, volunteerism and partnerships, we work together to build a community where everyone has an opportunity to thrive.

Our Mission

To promote inclusive community development by providing mentorship, educational support, dignity-centered sanitary care, protection from sexual violence, and empowerment programs for children, youth, and families in Mathare.

We engage diverse community members through active participation, volunteerism, and partnerships to create safe, healthy, and hopeful living environments.

Our Vision

A diverse, thriving, and empowered community where all children, youth, and families have equal opportunities, dignity, and support to achieve their full potential.

Our Programs

We empower vulnerable families and communities through education, health, humanitarian support, environmental conservation, and youth development.

🎓

Education & School Mentorship

We support children from vulnerable families through school mentorship and educational materials, helping learners stay motivated, develop their potential, and pursue their educational goals.

💚

Health & Dignity Support

We promote reproductive health awareness and provide sanitary pads to girls and young women, supporting dignity, confidence, and healthy development.

🛡️

Sexual Awareness & Protection

We create awareness on sexual health and protection, helping children, young people, and families understand their rights and make informed and safer choices.

🤝

Community Engagement & Talent Development

We engage communities and create opportunities for young people to discover, develop, and showcase their talents through sports, creative arts, leadership, and community activities.

🍽️

Feeding & Humanitarian Support

We provide feeding support and humanitarian assistance to vulnerable children, families, and people experiencing difficult living conditions, helping meet essential needs with compassion and dignity.

🏡

Orphanage & Family Outreach

We visit orphanages and reach vulnerable families with encouragement, essential support, and meaningful community outreach that brings hope and strengthens connections.

🌳

Environmental Conservation

We promote environmental responsibility through conservation activities, community awareness, tree planting, clean-up initiatives, and actions that help protect our environment for future generations.
